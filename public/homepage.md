# Sri Ashwini Physiotherapy Clinic

Sri Ashwini Physiotherapy Clinic is a physiotherapy clinic in Danavaipeta, Rajahmundry, Andhra Pradesh.

## About the Clinic

Sri Ashwini Physiotherapy Clinic provides personalised physiotherapy care focused on movement, strength, rehabilitation and helping patients improve their physical function.

The clinic serves patients in Rajahmundry and nearby areas.

## Services

- Electro Therapy
- Manual Therapy
- Cupping Therapy
- Exercise Therapy
- Dry Needling

## Conditions

Physiotherapy care is available for movement-related conditions including:

- Back pain
- Neck pain
- Knee pain
- Shoulder pain
- Sports injuries
